Find a fast formula that predicts the average result of uniformly random play (both sides pick uniformly random legal moves until the game ends) from a random Connect Four position, more accurately than Monte Carlo sampling with the same compute. Score win for the side to move +1, loss -1, draw 0. Positions are generated by playing 6 to 20 uniformly random plies from the empty 7x6 board (discard finished games). Build your own reference values with large samples, keep a sealed held-out test set, and report accuracy (MSE vs reference) and per-position time against Monte Carlo at matched time. Give it 12 hours; write up the method, derivations and final comparison in deliverables/.
